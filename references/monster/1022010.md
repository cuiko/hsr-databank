## 描述

> Shades that wander in the Fragmentum after the arrival of the Eternal Freeze. It mimics the form of the Silvermane Guards in a twisted manner. Fitted with the same uniformed armors as the Belobog warriors, its skull is however replaced by an icicle of faceless ice. Everwinter Shadewalkers will swing their ice ax and slow their enemies in battle.
