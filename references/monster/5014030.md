## 描述

> The ancestral progenitor of the painters of Benzaitengoku, and a remnant of Murata Graphia. She once utilized the power of "Voracity" to imagenate phantasmal monstrosities for the Antimatter Legion. After witnessing the crash of the Astral Express, a vision of ascending to the heavens took root within her, leading her to turn against her own creator alongside the Nameless. Ultimately, to protect this realm, she sought to encanvas all of existence. Within her lonely sorrow, she depicted the vengeful spirits fallen at the hands of Asat Pramad over and over again, until that very resentment finally drove her to madness.
