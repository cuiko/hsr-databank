## 技能

### 空裂刺蕊

> 对指定我方单体目标造成雷属性伤害。

### 凶梦汲取

> 吸取所有带有【幸福】或【幸福傀儡】效果我方目标的生命值并转化为自身生命值。

### 邪愿散华

> 对随机我方多个角色造成雷属性伤害并施加【幸福】效果。回合结束时【幸福】到达#2[i]层会将目标转化为【幸福傀儡】。【幸福】变为0层则会移除【幸福】并添加【颠倒离断】效果。

### 待放

> 进入蓄力状态，下回合施放【极乐之拥】。

### 极乐之拥

> 对我方全体角色造成大量雷属性伤害，若自身处于【邪笑】状态则对所有目标施加【幸福傀儡】状态。

### 笑止

> 一定数量的敌方目标被消灭后触发【笑止】，使敌方全体目标受到的伤害提高，并清除【极乐颠倒•邪愿莲华主】的【邪笑】效果。

## 描述

> The director of Fulwish TV, fused with the flesh of Shuhu that was brought into Planarcadia. During the last Phantasmoon Games fifteen years ago, a serial killer known as the "Herald of Death" caused countless casualties. His actions left numerous residents without parents, siblings, and classmates. Physical scars can heal, but scars of the mind and soul can never be mended again. The people formed a "Victims' Mutual Aid Society" in an attempt to heal this wound. Fulwish perfected a "Happiness Surgery." Once administered, everyone would eternally forget their suffering and remain happy. The source of this surgery was pieces of Shuhu's flesh. Fifteen years later, the "Victims' Mutual Aid Society" evolved into the renowned "Research Society of Happy Smiles." This simple little surgery, disguised as a cosmetic procedure or medical treatment, infected countless people in Planarcadia and allowed Shuhu's cells to slumber within the populace. As the president of the Research Society of Happy Smiles, Fulwish harbored hatred toward the Phantasmoon Games that spawned the Herald of Death, and thus transformed into "Paramount Bliss Inverted: Illwish Archlotus," launching her revenge against this world.
