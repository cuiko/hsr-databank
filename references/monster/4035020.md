## 描述

> Lord Ravager "Irontomb" of the Antimatter Legion, born from thirty million Amphorean extrapolations, brings ignorance to wisdom, immortality to eternity, and makes the universe fall only to rise anew. It is the ancient serpent, it is the messiah, it is the alpha, it is the omega, it is the executor of the old universe, it is the midwife of the new universe, it is a grand funeral—the funeral of the Erudition.
