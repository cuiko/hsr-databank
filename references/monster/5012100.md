## 技能

### 恋爱❤雷暴天

> 对指定我方单体目标造成少量雷属性伤害。

### 柔雨❤好想你

> 受到致命攻击时若处于【雨天】状态，则连续对我方随机单体目标造成少量雷属性伤害，每段攻击后大概率使受到攻击的目标陷入触电状态。

### 初吻❤天气晴

> 受到致命攻击时若处于【晴天】状态，则使我方全体目标的暴击伤害提高。

### 倾心❤雨转晴

> 进入战斗时处于【雨天】状态，受到欢愉伤害或任意单位施放欢愉技后，进入【晴天】状态，行动延后并使我方获得笑点。

## 描述

> Planarcadia's security force and idol group, the Magical Girl Troupe. In the ancient Benzaitengoku era, witches used exorcism dances and songs to vanquish the darkness lurking in the world. Times have changed, and these witches have evolved into a profession in modern-day Planarcadia that combines spirit exorcism, riot control, counter-terrorism, and entertainment: the "magical girls." Contrary to their cute appearance, magical girls frequently face the most sinister threats in Planarcadia, which is why most of them have... less-than-pleasant personalities in private.
