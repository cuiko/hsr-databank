## 描述

> A Voidranger that has successfully survived particle annihilation, the positive and negative particles in its body have temporarily achieved a strange balance, granting them more power than ordinary reavers. Basic combat units of the Antimatter Legion. They possess some intelligence and are able to control reavers within a small surrounding area.
