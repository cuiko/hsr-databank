## 技能

### 它是一切败者的僭主

> 【征服或被征服】不会行动。

### 独负此世全部的残伤

> 被对应【受征服】状态的目标攻击时，受到的伤害大幅提高。受到致命攻击时消耗【颁赐者，千军首，天谴之矛】的生命值。

## 描述

> The tip of its spear has drawn a boundless border. The people and soldiers within the border are marked as conquered, or ones to be conquered. The blood of the fearful will become honey for the divine body, and the souls of the defiant will walk into the palace of glory under the River of Souls.
