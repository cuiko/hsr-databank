## 描述

> In order to prevent antiparticles from dissipating and annihilating normal matter, the Antimatter Legion created two types of miniature ion traps for transportation, collectively referred to as Baryons and Antibaryons. Baryons have basic intelligence, and can clear the path according to the commands and summons of Voidrangers on the battlefield.
