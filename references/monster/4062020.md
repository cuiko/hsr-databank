## 描述

> A distorted monster born from the fusing of a memetic lifeform from the Garden of Recollection and the golden ichor of "Destruction." It has begun to take the shape of a dog. It is aggressive, showing that even with humanity evaporated, its instinct of following the Destruction manifests in this manner.
