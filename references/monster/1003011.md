## 技能

### 突击

> 对指定我方单体造成物理属性伤害。

### 穿刺

> 对指定我方单体造成大量物理属性伤害。

### 盾反

> 进入反击状态，在受到我方单体攻击后获得1个额外回合，对该目标造成物理属性伤害。

### 增援

> 召唤【银鬃近卫】或【银鬃射手】。

### 鼓劲

> 使敌方除【银鬃尉官（错误）】外的目标攻击力提高并立即行动。

## 描述

> A false projection produced by compromised codes. The referent entity is the leader of a squad of Belobog's Silvermane Guards. As long as there are remaining friendly units, Silvermane Lieutenants will persistently raise a giant shield during combat. Attacking a Silvermane Lieutenant with their shield raised will result in a fierce counterattack.
