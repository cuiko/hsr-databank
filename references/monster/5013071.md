## 描述

> The youngest member of the group, a model citizen who perfectly embodies the school conduct code and looks to the future with starry-eyed optimism. Yet, beneath his earnestness lies a trace of shyness and self-doubt. When something piques his interest, he erupts with a manic enthusiasm only to be overcome with mortification afterward for being a nuisance. "It's a promise, then. You protect the universe, and we'll protect Planarcadia." Cleaving through despair! A blade as sharp as steel! The undying edge — Cinderborne Warrior! Darkness wanes, and dawn awakens! The Daybreak Squadron arises!
