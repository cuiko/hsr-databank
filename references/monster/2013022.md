## 描述

> A law-enforcing aurumaton engraved with the Ten-Lords Martial Scriptures and taking orders from the Ten-Lords Commission. Living beings are susceptible to the suffering of the Five Skandhas, but gold and metal will never be swayed and will unwaveringly follow the Ten Lords' Edicts. These aurumaton law enforcers use their strength to subdue evils that have strayed from the natural order of life, and bring them to the Netherworld to meet their sentence. Their authority is sometimes even greater than that of a judge.
