## 技能

### 罪血呛吐

> 对指定我方单体及其相邻目标造成虚数属性伤害。

### 威慑警戒

> 进入蓄力状态并使自身造成的伤害提高，下次行动时施放【罪血涡旋】。蓄力期间受到攻击会使下次攻击造成的伤害提高。

### 罪血涡旋

> 对我方全体造成虚数属性伤害。

## 描述

> A distorted monster born from the fusing of a memetic lifeform from the Garden of Recollection and the golden ichor of "Destruction." It is in the rudimentary form of a flying snake, enough to intimidate its assailants. Its twisted rationality went completely mad in the black tide, and that intense self-defense impulse imperceptibly took on a fantastical hue.
