## 描述

> A somber warrior that accompanies the Judges of the Ten-Lords Commission to execute missions. Adorned with the black and white mask that symbolizes the netherworld envoy, they wield a guillotine blade, their strikes echoing the weighty and unyielding duty of the Ten-Lords Commission.
