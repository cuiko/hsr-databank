## 描述

> A member of the Disciples of Sanctus Medicus, the secret organization that had lain hidden in the Xianzhou Luofu. They pursue cultivation using tools and merge with them. They long to revive the lost secrets of Abundance, hoping to spur Xianzhou natives toward their next stage of evolution.
