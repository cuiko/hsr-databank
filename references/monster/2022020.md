## 技能

### 种丹

> 使指定我方单体陷入【引动雷劫】状态。

### 役雷

> 对指定我方单体造成少量雷属性伤害。

### 丹毁

> 受到致命攻击时，对所有陷入被自身添加的【引动雷劫】状态的我方目标造成雷属性伤害，并解除这些【引动雷劫】状态。

## 描述

> A member of the Disciples of Sanctus Medicus, the secret sect that hid within the Xianzhou Luofu.  With medicine, purify the heart. With elixir, transform one's body. They long to revive the ancient mysteries of Abundance, hoping to spur Xianzhou natives towards their next stage of evolution.
