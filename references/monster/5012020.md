## 技能

### 缉拿

> 对指定我方单体目标造成物理属性伤害。

### 护卫

> 对指定敌方单体附加【忠勇】。持有【忠勇】的目标在受到攻击时，治安忠犬将使此次攻击的伤害转移给自身，并使我方获得笑点。

## 描述

> An imagenae widely employed in public security and criminal investigations to assist combat units. The Department of Aberration Defense mass-produced them using the children's book The Casefiles of Wonder Dog Lassio as a template, ensuring that every imagenated dog is loyal and possesses a burning hatred for evil.
