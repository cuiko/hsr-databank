## 技能

### 读或死！

> 对指定我方单体造成少量物理属性伤害。

### 知识的诅咒

> 战斗开始时，处于【饱读感】状态，造成的伤害提高，受到的伤害降低。当受到欢愉伤害后，或当我方获得笑点并且拥有的笑点达到一定个数时，解除【饱读感】状态。

## 描述

> As the saying goes, "Buying books is like a landslide; reading them is like spinning silk." Bibliophiles are always buying new volumes yet rarely crack them open. The guilt of having "so much unread" and the self-deception that "I'll get to it someday" have woven together to create a vicious imagenae known as "Aggressive Reading Material."
