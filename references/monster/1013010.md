### 爆裂指令

> 召唤【自动机兵•蜘蛛】。

### 愤怒指令

> 有概率使我方全体陷入嘲讽状态。

### 过载指令

> 进入蓄力状态，下次行动对我方全体施放【剿灭指令】。

### 剿灭指令

> 对我方全体造成大量物理属性伤害。

## 描述

> An automaton pieced together with Old World technology. It has been reinforced with durable armor, so it can charge forward and protect the Silvermane Guards. In combat, Automaton Grizzlys will summon Automaton Spiders and unleash a powerful charged attack.
