## 描述

> A false projection produced by compromised codes. The referent entity is an automaton pieced together with Old World technology. It's been reinforced with durable armor to charge forward and protect the Silvermane Guards. In combat, Automaton Grizzlies (Bug) will send out a signal to summon Automaton Spiders and Beatles, and at the same time unleash powerful charged attacks.
